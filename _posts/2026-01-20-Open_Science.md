I have been officially appointed as an expert member of the **Working Group on Open Science** at the University of Turin. I have been appointed as an expert in "quantitative analysis of science, science policy and mapping of scientific systems".

The Working Group supports the development of open science practices at the university, contributing to the drafting of institutional policies and to the implementation of the [National Open Science Plan](https://www.mur.gov.it/sites/default/files/2022-06/Piano_Nazionale_per_la_Scienza_Aperta.pdf) (part of the National Research Program 2021-2027). The Working Group includes researchers from a wide array of disciplines, from life sciences to archaeology, from computer science to physics.

I am particularly interested in *open science and research evaluation* and *open data management*.
