A new paper is out! "SOPHIS: an open-access database for quantitative studies of contemporary philosophy of science", has been [published today](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11192-026-05591-4) in *Scientometrics* in open access!

The **SOPHIS** database described in the paper (acronym for **S**ocial **O**bservatory of **PHI**losophy of **S**cience) is the result of two years of hard work. It involved extensive manual data collection (we had to open and browse the PDFs of more than 6,000 articles), followed by painstaking and intensive data cleaning.
Both the paper and the [database](https://zenodo.org/doi/10.5281/zenodo.17277030) are now freely available in open access to the research community. We hope they will become a standard infrastructure for quantitative research in philosophy of science.

Last but not least, I’m especially proud of this paper because it is the first one I have co-authored with my wife, Selene, who brilliantly designed the graph version of SOPHIS!
