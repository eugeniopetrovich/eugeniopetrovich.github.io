Between April and July 2026, I will be DAAD Guest Professor at the Ruhr University Bochum in Germany! I will be hosted by the [Institute for Philosophy II](https://www.pe.ruhr-uni-bochum.de/philosophie/ii/institut/index.html.en) under the Chair of Social Epistemology and Philosophy of Science led by Prof. Dunja Šešelja. 

I will teach 3 courses for students of Philosophy and Cognitive Sciences:

- Data Science for Philosophy (30h, standard lectures)
- Scientometrics and Science Mapping (30h, standard lectures)
- Quantitative Studies of Philosophy (30h, block seminar)

Visit the [Teaching page](/teaching/) for courses' materials and updates.
