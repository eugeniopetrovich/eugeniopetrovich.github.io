**EDHIPHY** (**E**niched **D**ata for the **HI**story of **PH**ilosoph**Y**) is a database for quantitative history of philosophy in the Twentieth century that I developed during my post-doc at Tilburg University under the [Exiled Empiricists](https://exiledempiricists.wordpress.com/) project.

EDHIPHY is based on a new methodology that allows to extract *mentions* to philosophers from a corpus of text. Mentions were the common way of referencing ideas and arguments from fellow philosophers before the advent of standardized citations. 

In its current version, EDHIPHY covers 22,977 articles published in 12 Anglophone philosophy journals between 1890 and 1979. Its mention index includes 1,095,765 mention links, of which 93% are linked to specific philosophers, with an estimated precision of 82% to 91%.

Gregor E. Bös and Wessel Dankers have developed a beautiful [web application](https://edhiphy.org/) to navigate EDHIPHY data, and are currently maintaining it. [Tutorials](https://edhiphy.org/tutorials) on how to use the web application are available as well.

## Related publications

The rationale and technical implementation of EDHIPHY are described in the following article:

- Eugenio Petrovich, Sander Verhaegh, Gregor Bös, Claudia Cristalli, Fons Dewulf, Ties van Gemert & Nina IJdens: 'Bibliometrics beyond citations: introducing mention extraction and analysis', published 2024 in *Scientometrics* ([open access](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11192-024-05116-x)).

The following publication uses EDHIPHY to reconstruct the American reception of logical empiricism:

- Verhaegh, S., Petrovich, E. and Bös, G.E. (2025) ‘The American Reception of Logical Empiricism: A Mention-Based Bibliometric Analysis’, in F. Stadler and G. Schiemer (eds) *The Vienna Circle - History and Legacy*, ([doi](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-031-80568-4_7)).
